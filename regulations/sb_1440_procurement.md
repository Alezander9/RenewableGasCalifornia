# SB 1440 Procurement - Regulation

Senate Bill (SB) 1440 (Hueso, 2018) requires the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) to adopt biomethane procurement targets for California's investor-owned utilities (IOUs). These targets are designed to reduce short-lived climate pollutant emissions from organic waste, as mandated by [SB 1383](sb_1383_methane.md). The implementation of these targets is primarily governed by CPUC Decision (D.) 22-02-025 [5](#ref-5).

## SoCalGas Implementation
[SoCalGas](../companies/socalgas.md) is implementing its biomethane procurement plan in accordance with CPUC mandates to transition its gas supply toward renewable sources.

### Procurement Targets
*   **Short-term (by 2025):** SoCalGas is working toward a statewide goal of procuring 17.6 billion cubic feet (Bcf) of renewable natural gas (RNG) annually, specifically derived from organic waste diverted from landfills [1](#ref-1).
*   **Medium-term (by 2030):** The target is to procure RNG equal to 12.2% of bundled core gas demand (based on 2020 levels) [1](#ref-1).

### Solicitation Schedule
*   **Q2 2025 Request for Offers (RFO):** Issued on May 27, 2025, with a closing date of June 15, 2025. Contract execution for this solicitation is targeted for December 17, 2025 [2](#ref-2).
*   **Future Solicitation:** SoCalGas intends to hold subsequent solicitation events in 2026 to continue meeting SB 1440 requirements [2](#ref-2).

## PG&E Implementation
[PG&E](../companies/pge.md) has submitted a Draft Renewable Gas Procurement Plan (RGPP) to comply with SB 1440 and CPUC requirements.

### Procurement Targets
*   **Short-term (2025-2029):** PG&E's annual procurement target is 7.452 Bcf [3](#ref-3).
*   **Medium-term (2030-2040):** PG&E's annual procurement target increases to 27.745 Bcf [3](#ref-3).

### Implementation Strategy
*   **Procurement Methods:** PG&E utilizes a combination of competitive RFOs and bilateral negotiations to procure biomethane [3](#ref-3).
*   **Governance and Feedback:** A Procurement Advisory Group (PAG) provides informal feedback on PG&E's procurement strategy and supplier selection process [3](#ref-3).
*   **Cost Containment:** The implementation includes a three-tier Advice Letter approval process for contracts, based on the price per MMBtu relative to established market benchmarks [3](#ref-3).
*   **Current Status:** As of early 2026, PG&E's dedicated RNG solicitation portal lists future RFOs as "Coming soon" [4](#ref-4).
