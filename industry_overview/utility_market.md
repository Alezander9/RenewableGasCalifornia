# Utility Market - Overview

The utility market for biomethane in California is primarily driven by regulatory mandates that require investor-owned utilities (IOUs) to procure renewable gas [1](#ref-1).

## Key Regulations
*   **[SB 1440 Procurement](../regulations/sb_1440_procurement.md):** Mandates specific biomethane procurement targets for major utilities including [SoCalGas](../companies/socalgas.md) and [PG&E](../companies/pge.md) [1](#ref-1).

## Utility Implementation
Major utilities are implementing these mandates through competitive Request for Offers (RFOs) and bilateral negotiations to secure biomethane derived from organic waste [2](#ref-2) [3](#ref-3).
