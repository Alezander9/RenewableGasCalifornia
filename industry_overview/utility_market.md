# Utility Market - Overview

The utility market for biomethane in California is primarily driven by regulatory mandates that require investor-owned utilities (IOUs) to procure renewable gas.

## Key Regulations
*   **[SB 1440 Procurement](../regulations/sb_1440_procurement.md):** Mandates specific biomethane procurement targets for major utilities including SoCalGas and PG&E.

## Utility Implementation
Major utilities are implementing these mandates through competitive Request for Offers (RFOs) and bilateral negotiations to secure biomethane derived from organic waste.
