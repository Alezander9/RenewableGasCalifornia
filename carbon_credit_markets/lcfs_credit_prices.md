# LCFS Credit Prices - Analysis

Historical LCFS credit prices have shown significant volatility since the program's inception. Based on the California Air Resources Board (CARB) LCFS Data Dashboard, the following trends have been observed:

## Historical Price Trends

*   **2013-2015**: Prices started relatively low, generally remaining under $50 per metric ton (MT) [1](#ref-1).
*   **2016-2019**: Prices experienced a steady increase, rising from approximately $60/MT to over $150/MT [1](#ref-1).
*   **2020**: Prices reached a peak of approximately $200/MT [1](#ref-1).
*   **2021-2024**: Prices entered a downward trend, dropping to approximately $170/MT in 2021 and declining further to roughly $70/MT by early 2024 [1](#ref-1).
