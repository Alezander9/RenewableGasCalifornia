# LCFS Overview - Summary

The Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS) is a cornerstone of California's climate policy, creating a market-based system for carbon credits in the transportation sector. [1](#ref-1)

For a detailed breakdown of the regulatory history and the recent 2024/2025 amendments, see the main [LCFS Program](../regulations/lcfs_program.md) page.

## Market Context

The LCFS market allows producers of low-carbon fuels (such as RNG, electricity for EVs, and hydrogen) to generate credits that can be sold to high-carbon fuel providers who must meet annual carbon intensity targets. [1](#ref-1)

Key market drivers include:
*   Credit price volatility.
*   Regulatory shifts (e.g., the 2025 CI step-down). [2](#ref-2)
*   Growth in Zero Emission Vehicle (ZEV) infrastructure. [2](#ref-2)
