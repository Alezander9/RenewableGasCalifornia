# Book and Claim Accounting for RNG

Book-and-claim accounting allows for the separation of environmental attributes from the physical molecule of Renewable Natural Gas (RNG), enabling the attributes to be traded and claimed even if the physical gas is used elsewhere, provided both injection and withdrawal occur within a common carrier pipeline system.

## CARB LCFS Accounting Rules

The California Air Resources Board (CARB) maintains specific requirements for book-and-claim accounting under the [Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)](lcfs_overview.md), recently updated in the 2024 LCFS Final Regulation Order and Guidance 19-05.

### Core Principles
*   **Pipeline Injection:** To be eligible for book-and-claim accounting, biomethane must be injected into a common carrier pipeline system within North America [1](#ref-1).
*   **Three-Quarter Matching Limit:** Environmental attributes associated with RNG must be matched to physical fuel withdrawal or dispensing within a three-quarter time span. Attributes not claimed within three quarters of injection expire for LCFS purposes [1](#ref-1).
*   **Eligible Uses:** Book-and-claim is permitted for RNG used as:
    *   Bio-CNG, Bio-LNG, or Bio-L-CNG for transportation.
    *   Feedstock for renewable hydrogen production.
    *   Fuel for electricity generation via fuel cells for EV charging [1](#ref-1).

### 2024 'Directional Flow' Amendments
The 2024 amendments introduced "directional flow" requirements to ensure that RNG projects have a physical connection to the California market [1](#ref-1):
*   **Applicability:** These rules apply to biomethane projects that break ground after December 31, 2029.
*   **Physical Flow Rule:** Reporting entities must demonstrate that the pipeline delivery path physically flows from the injection point toward California at least 50% of the time on an annual basis.
*   **Enforcement Timeline:** 
    *   **Bio-CNG/LNG:** Enforcement begins January 1, 2041 (potentially accelerated to 2037 based on ZEV adoption).
    *   **EV Charging/Hydrogen:** Enforcement begins January 1, 2046.

### Documentation and Verification
Rigorous documentation is required to prevent double-counting and ensure environmental integrity:
*   **Evidence:** Pathway holders must provide unredacted monthly invoices showing RNG quantities and prices, as well as unredacted contracts for environmental attributes [1](#ref-1).
*   **Attestations:** Upstream parties must provide attestations confirming exclusive rights to the attributes and ensuring no other entity has claimed them in other programs, with the exception of the Federal RFS [1](#ref-1).
*   **Verification:** Pathways utilizing book-and-claim biomethane are subject to full verification and cannot defer the process [2](#ref-2).
