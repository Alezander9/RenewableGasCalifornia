# RFS RIN Market - Analysis

The Renewable Fuel Standard (RFS) program, administered by the EPA, requires a certain volume of renewable fuel to replace or reduce the quantity of petroleum-based transportation fuel, heating oil, or jet fuel. For [Renewable Natural Gas (RNG)](../OVERVIEW.md), the program generates Renewable Identification Numbers (RINs) that track compliance [2](#ref-2).

## RIN Categories for RNG

RNG primarily generates two types of RINs based on the feedstock source and the fuel's lifecycle greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions reduction [3](#ref-3):

*   **D3 (Cellulosic Biofuel):** Generated from cellulosic feedstocks such as [landfill gas](../industry_overview/feedstock_types.md), wastewater treatment sludge, and agricultural residues.
*   **D5 (Advanced Biofuel):** Generated from advanced feedstocks that do not meet the cellulosic definition but still achieve at least a 50% GHG reduction.

## RIN Generation Data (2024-2025)

RIN generation for RNG-derived fuels is a significant component of the D3 cellulosic biofuel category. The following table summarizes RIN generation for various RNG-related fuel types in 2024 and 2025.

| Year | RIN Category | Fuel Type | RIN Quantity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2024 | D3 (Cellulosic) | Renewable Compressed Natural Gas | 880,960,893 |
| 2024 | D3 (Cellulosic) | Renewable Liquefied Natural Gas | 82,642,184 |
| 2024 | D3 (Cellulosic) | Renewable Natural Gas - RNG | 9,820,158 |
| 2024 | D5 (Advanced) | Renewable Compressed Natural Gas | 1,252,569 |
| 2025 | D3 (Cellulosic) | Renewable Natural Gas - RNG | 1,096,653,142 |
| 2025 | D3 (Cellulosic) | Renewable Compressed Natural Gas | 1,878,401 |
| 2025 | D3 (Cellulosic) | Renewable Compressed Natural Gas - CNG | 694,019 |
| 2025 | D5 (Advanced) | Renewable Natural Gas - RNG | 387,420 |
| 2025 | D5 (Advanced) | Renewable Compressed Natural Gas - CNG | 547,270 |
| 2025 | D5 (Advanced) | Renewable Compressed Natural Gas | 14,085 |

*Note: The 2025 data reflects the full year as reported in the EPA's December 2025 summary [1](#ref-1).*
