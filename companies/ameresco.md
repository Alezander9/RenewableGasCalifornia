# Ameresco - Overview

Ameresco has developed multiple landfill gas-to-energy (LFGTE) and renewable natural gas (RNG) projects in California. [1](#ref-1)

## California Landfill Gas-to-Energy Projects

### Keller Canyon RNG Plant
*   **Location:** Pittsburg, CA (Keller Canyon Landfill) [1](#ref-1)
*   **Capacity:** Processes 4,500 scfm of LFG; delivers ~1 BCF of RNG annually; 11.7 MWe Energy Asset [1](#ref-1)
*   **Partners:** Republic Services, PG&E [1](#ref-1)
*   **Status:** Commercial operations began September 2024 [1](#ref-1)

### Keller Canyon Landfill Gas-to-Electricity Project
*   **Location:** Pittsburg, CA (Keller Canyon Landfill) [2](#ref-2)
*   **Capacity:** 3.8 MW capacity; powers nearly 2,200 homes [2](#ref-2)
*   **Partners:** Republic Services, City of Palo Alto, City of Alameda [2](#ref-2)

### Johnson Canyon Landfill Gas-to-Energy Project
*   **Location:** Gonzales, CA (Johnson Canyon Landfill) [3](#ref-3)
*   **Capacity:** 1.4 MW capacity; powers nearly 1,000 homes [3](#ref-3)
*   **Partners:** Salinas Valley Solid Waste Authority (SVSWA), City of Palo Alto (PPA) [3](#ref-3)
*   **Status:** Completed September 2013 [3](#ref-3)

### Butte County Neal Road Landfill Gas-to-Energy Project
*   **Location:** Paradise, CA (Neal Road Recycling and Waste Facility) [4](#ref-4)
*   **Capacity:** 2.2 MW (projected to expand to 4.3 MW); powers over 1,300 homes [4](#ref-4)
*   **Partners:** Butte County [4](#ref-4)
*   **Status:** Completed December 2012 [4](#ref-4)

### Vasco Road Landfill Gas-to-Electricity (LFGTE) Plant
*   **Location:** Livermore, CA (Vasco Road Landfill) [5](#ref-5)
*   **Capacity:** 4.3 MW capacity; powers homes in Santa Clara and the SF Bay Area [5](#ref-5)
*   **Partners:** Republic Services, Silicon Valley Power [5](#ref-5)
*   **Status:** Operations commenced late 2012 [5](#ref-5)

### Foothill Landfill Gas-to-Energy Project
*   **Location:** Linden, CA (Foothill Landfill) [6](#ref-6)
*   **Capacity:** 4.3 MW capacity; powers more than 2,600 local homes [6](#ref-6)
*   **Partners:** San Joaquin County, City of Palo Alto (PPA) [6](#ref-6)
*   **Status:** Completed November 2014 [6](#ref-6)

### Forward Landfill Gas-to-Energy Project
*   **Location:** Manteca, CA (Forward Landfill) [7](#ref-7)
*   **Capacity:** 4.3 MW capacity [7](#ref-7)
*   **Partners:** Republic Services, Silicon Valley Power (SVP) [7](#ref-7)
*   **Status:** Completed September 2014 [7](#ref-7)

### Buena Vista Landfill Gas-to-Energy Plant
*   **Location:** Watsonville, CA (Buena Vista Landfill) [8](#ref-8)
*   **Capacity:** 3.2 MW capacity; powers 3,000 homes [8](#ref-8)
*   **Partners:** Santa Cruz County, City of Palo Alto, Alameda Power & Telecom [8](#ref-8)
*   **Status:** Operational as of February 2006 [8](#ref-8)
