# Ameresco - Overview

Ameresco (NYSE: AMRC) is a clean technology integrator and renewable energy asset developer, owner, and operator.

## Financial Performance

### Renewable Natural Gas Revenue
Ameresco reports revenue from renewable natural gas (RNG) within its **Renewable Fuels** segment. For the third quarter of 2025 (three months ended September 30), the company generated $37.7 million in revenue from this segment. For the first nine months of 2025, RNG-related revenue totaled $118.7 million [1](#ref-1).

### Project Backlog
As of September 30, 2025, Ameresco's project backlog reached significant levels:
*   **Total Project Backlog**: $5.14 billion [1](#ref-1)
*   **12-Month Project Backlog**: $1.25 billion [1](#ref-1)

The 12-month backlog represents the estimated value of work under signed contracts and awarded projects that is expected to be recognized as revenue over the following twelve months [1](#ref-1).
