# Sevana Bioenergy - Overview

## Assets

### Rialto Bioenergy Facility (RBF)
In June 2024, Sevana Bioenergy acquired the Rialto Bioenergy Facility for $20 million at auction following the bankruptcy of a subsidiary of [Anaergia](anaergia.md) [1](#ref-1) [2](#ref-2). Located in Rialto, California, the facility has a processing capacity of 1,000 tons per day (700 tons of food waste and 300 tons of municipal biosolids) and produces renewable natural gas (RNG) and fertilizer [1](#ref-1). Sevana intends to ramp up production and throughput at the plant [1](#ref-1).
