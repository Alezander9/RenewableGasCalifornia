# CR&R Environmental Services

CR&R Environmental Services operates one of the world's largest anaerobic digestion (AD) facilities in Perris, California. The facility is a flagship project for organic waste processing and renewable natural gas (RNG) production.

## Perris Anaerobic Digestion Facility

### Overview
The facility, located at 1706 Goetz Rd, Perris, CA 92570, is designed to process food scraps, green waste, and Fats, Oils, and Grease (FOGs) [1](#ref-1) [3](#ref-3). It is recognized as the largest food and green waste anaerobic digester in the United States and has been described as the "largest organic-waste eating machine in the world" [3](#ref-3).

### Capacity and Expansion
The facility is being developed in four phases. At full completion, it is designed to process **335,000 tons per year** of organic waste [1](#ref-1) [3](#ref-3). Each of the four phases contributes approximately 83,600 tons per year of capacity [3](#ref-3).

### Technology
The facility employs **Eisenmann "mixed plug-flow"** anaerobic digestion technology for waste processing [2](#ref-2) [3](#ref-3). For gas refining and cleaning, it utilizes **Greenlane Biogas** technology to produce pipeline-quality RNG [2](#ref-2) [3](#ref-3).

### Energy Production and Infrastructure
- **RNG Output:** At full capacity, the facility generates enough RNG to displace **4 million gallons of diesel fuel** annually [1](#ref-1) [3](#ref-3).
- **Utility Interconnect:** The site features the only RNG interconnect in California with SoCalGas, allowing direct injection of renewable gas into the utility's pipeline [1](#ref-1) [4](#ref-4).
- **Fleet Integration:** The RNG produced currently fuels 320 of CR&R's collection vehicles. Upon full expansion, the facility is expected to fuel the company's entire fleet of 900 vehicles [3](#ref-3).

### Byproducts
The digestion process produces approximately **260,000 tons of nutrient-rich natural fertilizer** per year as a byproduct [3](#ref-3).
