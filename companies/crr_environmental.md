# CR&R Environmental Services

CR&R Environmental Services is a waste management company operating in Southern California, specializing in waste collection and the conversion of organic waste into Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) [1](#ref-1). Its facility in Perris is one of the world's largest anaerobic digestion (AD) plants [2](#ref-2) [4](#ref-4).

## Waste Collection Contracts

CR&R holds waste collection and processing contracts with over 30 municipalities in Southern California [5](#ref-5).

### Orange County
Contracted cities include: Anaheim, Brea, Buena Park, Cypress, Dana Point, Fountain Valley, Fullerton, Garden Grove, Huntington Beach, Irvine, La Habra, La Palma, Laguna Beach, Laguna Hills, Laguna Niguel, Laguna Woods, Lake Forest, Mission Viejo, Newport Beach, Orange, Placentia, Rancho Santa Margarita, San Clemente, San Juan Capistrano, Santa Ana, Stanton, Tustin, and Yorba Linda [5](#ref-5).

- **Laguna Niguel**: In 2023, CR&R was established as a Direct Service Provider of RNG for the city to meet [SB 1383](../regulations/sb_1383_methane.md) requirements. The collection vehicles serving Laguna Niguel are powered by RNG produced at CR&R's Perris facility [3](#ref-3).

### Riverside County
Contracted cities include: Perris, Temecula, San Jacinto, Canyon Lake, Lake Elsinore, Hemet, Calimesa, and Wildomar [5](#ref-5).
Perris and Temecula were "front runner" franchise cities that supported the early development of CR&R's anaerobic digestion project [1](#ref-1).

### Other Locations
CR&R also serves Colton and the Heber Public Utility District [5](#ref-5) [6](#ref-6).

## Perris Anaerobic Digestion Facility

The facility, located at 1706 Goetz Rd, Perris, CA 92570, converts residential yard trimmings and food waste into pipeline-quality RNG [4](#ref-4). It is recognized as the largest food and green waste anaerobic digester in the United States [4](#ref-4).

### Technology and Infrastructure
- **Technology**: The plant utilizes **Eisenmann "mixed plug-flow"** (high-solids) anaerobic digestion technology and a **Greenlane Biogas** water-wash system for gas upgrading [2](#ref-2) [4](#ref-4) [5](#ref-5).
- **Utility Interconnect**: The site features the only RNG interconnect in California with [SoCalGas](socalgas.md), allowing direct injection of renewable gas into the utility's pipeline [2](#ref-2) [7](#ref-7). This involves a 1.4-mile pipeline interconnect [1](#ref-1).
- **Fleet Integration**: The RNG produced currently fuels 320 of CR&R's collection vehicles. Upon full expansion, the facility is expected to fuel the company's entire fleet of 900 vehicles [4](#ref-4).

### Capacity and Production
- **Phased Development**: The facility is being developed in four phases. Phase I is permitted for 83,600 tons of organic waste per year [1](#ref-1) [4](#ref-4).
- **Full Capacity**: At full completion, the facility is designed to process **335,000 tons per year** of organic waste [2](#ref-2) [4](#ref-4).
- **RNG Output**: At full capacity, the facility generates enough RNG to displace **4 million gallons of diesel fuel** annually [2](#ref-2) [4](#ref-4).
- **Byproducts**: The digestion process produces approximately **260,000 tons of nutrient-rich natural fertilizer** per year as a byproduct at full capacity [2](#ref-2) (early estimates suggested 65,000 tons for initial phases [4](#ref-4)).
