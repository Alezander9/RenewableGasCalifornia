# BioFuels Energy - Overview

## SoCalGas Mentions

BioFuels Energy LLC is identified as a partner in a SoCalGas project at the Point Loma Wastewater Treatment Plant, which involves upgrading biogas to pipeline-quality renewable natural gas (RNG) for injection into the SoCalGas system [1].

In searches of SoCalGas public records:
* **CNG/RNG Vendor Directory**: BioFuels Energy LLC was not found in the public-facing vendor directory as of February 2026 [2].
* **Interconnection Queue**: The company was not found in the public interconnection queue documents searched on the SoCalGas website [3].
