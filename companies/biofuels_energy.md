# BioFuels Energy - Overview

BioFuels Energy, LLC is a renewable energy company based in California that specializes in the development of biomethane purification plants and fuel cell energy projects. The company is headquartered in Encinitas, California [1](#ref-1).

## Encinitas Presence

While BioFuels Energy, LLC is headquartered at 2210 Encinitas Blvd, Suite P, Encinitas, CA 92024, the company does not have any operational renewable energy projects (such as fuel cells or purification plants) located within the city limits of Encinitas [1](#ref-1) [2](#ref-2). Local permit searches and company records indicate that the Encinitas location serves exclusively as the administrative and corporate headquarters [3](#ref-3).

## Projects in California

BioFuels Energy has developed and operates several significant projects across California, primarily in the San Diego and Long Beach areas [4](#ref-4) [5](#ref-5).

### San Diego
*   **Point Loma Wastewater Treatment Plant:** A biomethane gas purification plant that produces pipeline-quality renewable natural gas (RNG) from digester gas for injection into the [SoCalGas](socalgas.md) system [4](#ref-4).
*   **South Bay Water Reclamation Plant:** A 1.4 MW fuel cell plant that offsets utility power for the facility [4](#ref-4).
*   **Sheraton San Diego Hotel and Marina:** A 500 kW fuel cell plant providing base-load electricity and heat [4](#ref-4).
*   **University of California San Diego (UCSD):** A 2.8 MW fuel cell plant located in La Jolla, which was the largest of its kind at a university at the time of installation [4](#ref-4).

### Long Beach
*   **Aquarium of the Pacific:** A 300 kW fuel cell system providing clean energy for the facility [5](#ref-5).

### San Marcos
*   **California State University San Marcos:** Involved in energy projects on the university campus [5](#ref-5).

## SoCalGas Mentions

In searches of [SoCalGas](socalgas.md) public records:
* **CNG/RNG Vendor Directory**: BioFuels Energy LLC was not found in the public-facing vendor directory as of February 2026 [6](#ref-6).
* **Interconnection Queue**: The company was not found in the public interconnection queue documents searched on the SoCalGas website [7](#ref-7).
