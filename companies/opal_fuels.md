# OPAL Fuels - Overview

OPAL Fuels Inc. (NASDAQ: OPAL) is a vertically integrated renewable natural gas (RNG) producer and distributor [2](#ref-2). The company focuses on the capture of methane from landfills and livestock farms to produce RNG for use as a [transportation fuel](../industry_overview/transportation_market.md) and for other energy applications [1](#ref-1).

## Project Pipeline

As of late 2025, OPAL Fuels has significantly expanded its RNG production capacity and infrastructure:

*   **Atlantic RNG Project:** Commenced commercial operations in October 2025, contributing approximately 0.3 million MMBtu to annual design capacity (reflecting OPAL's 50% ownership) [1](#ref-1).
*   **CMS Concord RNG (North Carolina):** Construction began in October 2025. The project is expected to provide 0.7 million MMBtu in annual design capacity (OPAL's 70% share) [1](#ref-1).
*   **Burlington and Cottonwood RNG Projects:** Both projects are expected to begin commercial operations in 2026, with a combined aggregate annual design capacity of 1.1 million MMBtu net to OPAL [1](#ref-1).
*   **Kirby RNG Project (California):** Expected to commence commercial operations in 2027, representing 0.7 million MMBtu (100% ownership) [1](#ref-1).
*   **Dairy Projects:** Development of the Hilltop and Vander Schaaf dairy projects remains delayed due to ongoing contractor disputes [1](#ref-1).
*   **Fueling Stations:** As of September 30, 2025, OPAL operated 47 fueling stations, with an additional 16 stations under construction [1](#ref-1).

## Financial Performance and Environmental Attributes

OPAL's revenue is heavily influenced by the sale of RNG and its associated environmental attributes, including [Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)](../regulations/lcfs_program.md) credits and [Renewable Identification Numbers (RINs)](../carbon_credit_markets/rfs_rin_market.md) [1](#ref-1).

*   **Quarterly Revenue:** In Q3 2025, OPAL reported total revenue of $83.4 million, with RNG fuel revenue accounting for $22.9 million [1](#ref-1).
*   **Environmental Attribute Monetization:** As of September 30, 2025, the value of "RNG Pending Monetization"—representing produced but unsold environmental attributes, including LCFS credits—stood at $14.7 million [1](#ref-1).
*   **Investment Tax Credits (ITC):** OPAL has actively utilized the [Inflation Reduction Act's](../regulations/federal_tax_credits_ira.md) tax incentives. In 2025, the company completed its fourth sale of ITCs, bringing total year-to-date proceeds from these sales to over $40 million [1](#ref-1).
*   **Production Tax Credits:** The company expected to begin recognizing 45Z production tax credits starting in Q4 2025 [1](#ref-1).
