# OPAL Fuels

OPAL Fuels Inc. (NASDAQ: OPAL) is a vertically integrated producer and distributor of [renewable natural gas (RNG)](../industry_overview/OVERVIEW.md), employing a "waste-to-energy" model that manages the entire lifecycle of [methane capture](../regulations/sb_1383_methane.md) and fuel dispensing [1](#ref-1) [7](#ref-7). The company focuses on the capture of methane from landfills and livestock farms to produce RNG for use as a [transportation fuel](../industry_overview/transportation_market.md) and for other energy applications [3](#ref-3).

## Vertically Integrated Business Model

The company operates across the entire RNG value chain, from upstream production to downstream distribution [2](#ref-2).

### Upstream Production
OPAL Fuels captures methane emissions from landfills and dairy farms that would otherwise be released into the atmosphere [1](#ref-1). This captured biogas is processed at on-site facilities where it is cleaned and purified into pipeline-quality RNG [2](#ref-2).
*   **Operating Scale:** As of Q3 2025, the company had 12 operating projects with a total annual design capacity of 9.1 million MMBtu [3](#ref-3).
*   **Specific Projects:** Significant operations include the Paloma Dairy project in Arizona and the Atlantic RNG project [3](#ref-3) [4](#ref-4).

### Downstream Distribution
The company designs, builds, owns, and operates custom [RNG fueling stations](../industry_overview/infrastructure.md) for [heavy-duty truck fleets](../industry_overview/transportation_market.md) [5](#ref-5).
*   **Station Network:** OPAL Fuels has completed over 350 fueling station projects [4](#ref-4). As of September 30, 2025, the company operated 47 fueling stations directly, with an additional 16 stations under construction [3](#ref-3).
*   **Fueling Volume:** In Q3 2025, the company sold or serviced 38.9 million Gallons of Gasoline Equivalent (GGEs) of fuel, with RNG accounting for 20.4 million GGEs [3](#ref-3).
*   **Service Model:** They provide a "no capital investment" model for fleet operators, which includes 24/7 service and maintenance [5](#ref-5).

## Project Pipeline

As of late 2025, OPAL Fuels has significantly expanded its RNG production capacity and infrastructure:

*   **Atlantic RNG Project:** Commenced commercial operations in October 2025, contributing approximately 0.3 million MMBtu to annual design capacity (reflecting OPAL's 50% ownership) [3](#ref-3).
*   **CMS Concord RNG (North Carolina):** Construction began in October 2025. The project is expected to provide 0.7 million MMBtu in annual design capacity (OPAL's 70% share) [3](#ref-3).
*   **Burlington and Cottonwood RNG Projects:** Both projects are expected to begin commercial operations in 2026, with a combined aggregate annual design capacity of 1.1 million MMBtu net to OPAL [3](#ref-3).
*   **Kirby RNG Project (California):** Expected to commence commercial operations in 2027, representing 0.7 million MMBtu (100% ownership) [3](#ref-3).
*   **Dairy Projects:** Development of the Hilltop and Vander Schaaf dairy projects remains delayed due to ongoing contractor disputes [3](#ref-3).

## Financial Performance and Environmental Attributes

OPAL's revenue is heavily influenced by the sale of RNG and its associated environmental attributes, including [Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)](../regulations/lcfs_program.md) credits and [Renewable Identification Numbers (RINs)](../carbon_credit_markets/rfs_rin_market.md) [3](#ref-3).

*   **Quarterly Revenue:** In Q3 2025, OPAL reported total revenue of $83.4 million, with RNG fuel revenue accounting for $22.9 million [3](#ref-3).
*   **Environmental Attribute Monetization:** As of September 30, 2025, the value of "RNG Pending Monetization"—representing produced but unsold environmental attributes, including LCFS credits—stood at $14.7 million [3](#ref-3).
*   **Investment Tax Credits (ITC):** OPAL has actively utilized the [Inflation Reduction Act's](../regulations/federal_tax_credits_ira.md) tax incentives. In 2025, the company completed its fourth sale of ITCs, bringing total year-to-date proceeds from these sales to over $40 million [3](#ref-3).
*   **Production Tax Credits:** The company expected to begin recognizing 45Z production tax credits starting in Q4 2025 [3](#ref-3).

## Integration Benefits
By controlling both production and distribution, OPAL Fuels ensures a reliable RNG supply and manages the [carbon credit lifecycle](../carbon_credit_markets/OVERVIEW.md) [2](#ref-2). This integration allows them to offer fuel at lower costs than diesel by passing incentives directly to fleet operators [5](#ref-5).
