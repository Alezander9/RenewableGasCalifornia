# Hitachi Zosen Inova - Overview

Hitachi Zosen Inova (now Kanadevia Inova) is a global leader in energy-from-waste (EfW) and renewable gas technology. One of its key projects in the United States is the Kompogas SLO facility.

## Projects

### Kompogas SLO (San Luis Obispo, CA)
Kompogas SLO is a state-of-the-art dry anaerobic digestion facility located in San Luis Obispo, California [1](#ref-1).

*   **Location:** 4300 Old Santa Fe Rd., San Luis Obispo, CA 93401 [3](#ref-3)
*   **Operational Date:** September 2018 [2](#ref-2)
*   **Technology:** The plant utilizes Kompogas® dry anaerobic digestion technology with a PF1800 steel digester [1](#ref-1).
*   **Waste Capacity:** The facility processes approximately 42,000 US tons per year of source-separated organic waste, including food and green waste [1](#ref-1).
*   **Energy Production:** [1](#ref-1)
    *   Biogas: 2,900,000 Nm³/a
    *   Electricity: 6,321,000 kWh/a
    *   Biomethane: 6,200,000 kWh/a
*   **Business Model:** Developed under a Design, Build, Finance, Own, Operate (DBFOO) model [1](#ref-1).
*   **Funding:** The project received a $4 million grant from CalRecycle and a $4 million grant from the California Energy Commission (CEC) EPIC program [1](#ref-1).
