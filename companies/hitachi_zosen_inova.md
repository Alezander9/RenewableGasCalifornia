# Hitachi Zosen Inova (Kanadevia Inova)

Hitachi Zosen Inova (HZI), recently rebranded as Kanadevia Inova, is a global leader in energy-from-waste (EfW) and renewable gas technologies. In California, the company is primarily known for its **Kompogas®** dry anaerobic digestion technology [1](#ref-1).

## Kompogas® Technology

Kompogas® is a continuous dry anaerobic digestion process designed for the treatment of organic waste.

- **Process Type:** Continuous dry anaerobic digestion using a horizontal plug-flow reactor [3](#ref-3).
- **Temperature:** Thermophilic operation (approx. 55°C / 131°F), which ensures waste sanitization [3](#ref-3).
- **Retention Time:** Typically around 14 days in the digester [3](#ref-3).
- **Agitation:** A robust horizontal agitator with plow-shaped blades ensures optimal mixing and biogas release [3](#ref-3).
- **Inoculation:** The system uses a patented process where a portion of the digestate is recirculated to the inlet to inoculate incoming fresh waste [3](#ref-3).
- **Outputs:** The process produces biogas (for electricity, heat, or RNG), liquid fertilizer, and high-quality compost [3](#ref-3).

## California Projects

### Kompogas SLO (San Luis Obispo)

The Kompogas SLO facility is the first of its kind in the United States and serves as a flagship project for HZI in California [1](#ref-1).

- **Location:** San Luis Obispo, California [1](#ref-1).
- **Operational Status:** Commenced operations in September 2018 [2](#ref-2).
- **Project Model:** Design, Build, Finance, Own, Operate (DBFOO) [1](#ref-1).
- **Capacity:** Processes approximately 42,000 US tons per year of green and food waste [1](#ref-1).
- **Energy Generation:**
  - **Biogas:** Produces roughly 2,900,000 Nm³/a of biogas [1](#ref-1).
  - **Electricity:** Generates approximately 6,321,000 kWh/a of renewable electricity [1](#ref-1).
- **Future Expansion:** In partnership with Char Tech, HZI is implementing a green hydrogen project at the SLO site. This expansion aims to process 18,000 tonnes/year of solid anaerobic digestate into 1,320 tonnes/year of green hydrogen and 2,800 tonnes/year of biocarbon [4](#ref-4).
