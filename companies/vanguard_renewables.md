# Vanguard Renewables - Overview

Vanguard Renewables is a developer and operator of dairy-farm based anaerobic digestion and organic waste-to-[renewable natural gas (RNG)](../industry_overview/OVERVIEW.md) projects. The company, a portfolio company of BlackRock, is known for its "Farm Powered" model, which integrates dairy farming with food waste recycling [1](#ref-1)[5](#ref-5).

## Expansion and Market Focus

### Strategic Partnerships
In April 2024, Vanguard Renewables entered into a 50-50 joint venture with TotalEnergies to develop, own, and operate farm-based RNG projects. This partnership aims to leverage Vanguard's expertise in organic waste management and TotalEnergies' experience in energy markets to accelerate the deployment of RNG facilities across the U.S. [1](#ref-1).

### California Market
California represents a critical market for Vanguard Renewables, primarily driven by the state's [Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)](../regulations/lcfs_program.md) [2](#ref-2). The LCFS provides a market for RNG produced at Vanguard's facilities nationwide, supporting the economic viability of its farm-based projects [2](#ref-2). The company's expansion strategy explicitly targets major markets like California to support the decarbonization of the energy grid [3](#ref-3).

### Growth Targets
As of March 2024, Vanguard Renewables operates in eight states and has established a goal to reach 100 operating facilities by 2028 [2](#ref-2).

## Farm Powered Strategic Alliance (FPSA)

The Farm Powered Strategic Alliance (FPSA) was founded in 2020 by Vanguard Renewables, Unilever, Starbucks, and Dairy Farmers of America. The alliance aims to reduce food waste, increase organic waste recycling, and produce renewable energy to support regenerative agriculture and the decarbonization of the food and beverage industry [4](#ref-4).

### Membership

The alliance includes a broad range of members committed to sustainability goals:

*   **Founding Members:** Vanguard Renewables, Unilever, Starbucks, and Dairy Farmers of America [4](#ref-4).
*   **National Members:** Agropur, AstraZeneca, BevProSDHL, Cabot, Chobani, Danone, FoodTank, Foremost, Great Lakes Cheese, Johnsonville, Milk Specialties Global, Polar, Schreiber, Smithfield, and Vermont Creamery [4](#ref-4).
*   **Regional Members (Northeast):** 88 Acres, CFES Seafoods, Fancy Pants, New England Natural Bakers, and Sugarman's [4](#ref-4).

## Dairy Digester Model (The 'Farm Powered' Process)

Vanguard Renewables utilizes a co-digestion model that combines traditional dairy manure management with food waste diversion [5](#ref-5).

### Technical Process

1.  **Inputs:** The process combines dairy manure from host farms with inedible food and beverage waste, which can be in liquid or solid/packaged form [6](#ref-6).
2.  **Depackaging:** Specialized facilities process packaged goods to separate organic material from packaging, ensuring a clean feedstock for the anaerobic digesters [5](#ref-5).
3.  **Anaerobic Digestion:** Organic materials are broken down by microorganisms in an oxygen-free environment within farm-based [digesters](../industry_overview/feedstock_types.md) [5](#ref-5).
4.  **Outputs:**
    *   **Renewable Natural Gas (RNG):** Methane is captured, refined, and injected into the natural gas grid [5](#ref-5).
    *   **Liquid Digestate:** A nutrient-rich, low-carbon fertilizer used by host farms to replace chemical fertilizers and improve soil health [5](#ref-5).
    *   **Animal Bedding:** Solid byproducts are processed into high-quality, odorless bedding for dairy cows [6](#ref-6).
