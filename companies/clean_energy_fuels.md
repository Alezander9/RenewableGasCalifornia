# Clean Energy Fuels - Overview

Clean Energy Fuels (CLNE) is a leading provider of renewable natural gas (RNG) for the transportation industry in North America. The company has transitioned from being primarily a natural gas distributor to a major RNG producer and supplier. [4](#ref-4)

## Market Position and RNG Sales
Clean Energy Fuels is the largest provider of RNG under the California [Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)](../regulations/lcfs_program.md) program [5](#ref-5). The company has seen significant growth in its RNG sales, which now constitute the vast majority of its vehicle fuel sales. [4](#ref-4)

*   **2025 Performance:** In the third quarter of 2025, the company reported selling **61.3 million gallons** of RNG, a 3% increase year-over-year [7](#ref-7). For the first half of 2025, total RNG sales had reached **112.0 million gallons** [1](#ref-1).
*   **2024 Annual Sales:** The company sold **236.7 million GGEs** of RNG in 2024, representing approximately 89% of its total vehicle fuel sales [4](#ref-4).
*   **Station Network:** Clean Energy Fuels maintains the largest network of natural gas fueling stations in North America, operating or supplying **over 200 stations in California** and over 600 stations across the United States and Canada as of late 2024 [4](#ref-4).

## 'Redeem' Brand
"Redeem" is Clean Energy's brand for its renewable natural gas fuel. [2](#ref-2) [3](#ref-3)
*   **Definition:** Launched as the first commercially available RNG vehicle fuel, Redeem is derived entirely from organic waste streams such as landfills, dairy farms, and sewage treatment plants [2](#ref-2) [3](#ref-3).
*   **Carbon Intensity:** According to California Air Resources Board (CARB) estimates, Redeem can reduce carbon emissions by up to **90%** compared to diesel or gasoline. When sourced specifically from dairy waste, it can even have a negative carbon intensity [2](#ref-2).

## Production Projects and Partnerships
The company is actively developing RNG production facilities, often through joint ventures, to increase its internal supply of RNG from dairy farms and other organic waste sites [4](#ref-4).

### Key Partnerships and Joint Ventures
*   **TotalEnergies:** A 50/50 joint venture focused on developing carbon-negative RNG production and infrastructure in the United States. TotalEnergies initially provided $100 million in equity, with the potential for this commitment to increase to $400 million [5](#ref-5).
*   **BP:** A 50/50 joint venture dedicated to developing RNG projects at dairy farms. BP initially committed $50 million and Clean Energy Fuels committed $30 million to this partnership [6](#ref-6). This JV followed BP's 2017 acquisition of Clean Energy's upstream RNG business for $155 million [6](#ref-6).
*   **[Maas Energy Works](maas_energy_works.md):** A joint venture focused on the development of dairy RNG projects. As of late 2025, multiple projects under this partnership were either under construction or recently completed [7](#ref-7).
*   **Pioneer Clean Fleet Solutions:** A strategic investment aimed at expanding the company's reach in clean fleet services [7](#ref-7).

### Production Capacity and Growth
*   **Facility Capacity:** Historical data indicates that the company's production facilities have targeted capacities around **20,000 GGE per day** per facility [2](#ref-2).
*   **2026 Growth Outlook:** Clean Energy Fuels anticipates a "near doubling" of its RNG production in 2026 as new dairy projects become operational [9](#ref-9).

## Financial Outlook and LCFS Impact
The company's financial performance is closely tied to the [LCFS](../regulations/lcfs_program.md) program and other carbon credit markets:

*   **LCFS Revenue:** In Q3 2025, Clean Energy Fuels reported $3.1 million in LCFS credit revenue, an increase from $1.9 million in Q3 2024 [7](#ref-7). For the first nine months of 2025, total LCFS revenue reached $9.7 million [7](#ref-7).
*   **Credit Dynamics:** Total revenue from [RINs](../carbon_credit_markets/rfs_rin_market.md) and LCFS credits combined was $11.4 million in Q3 2025, down from $13.0 million in Q3 2024, primarily due to lower RIN prices [7](#ref-7).
*   **2025 Guidance:** The company has provided a 2025 Adjusted EBITDA guidance range of $60 million to $65 million [8](#ref-8).
