# Clean Energy Fuels - RNG Production

Clean Energy Fuels (CLNE) is a leading provider of renewable natural gas (RNG) for the transportation industry in North America. The company has transitioned from being primarily a natural gas distributor to a major RNG producer and supplier.

## RNG Production and Sales Capacity

### RNG Sales Volumes
Clean Energy Fuels has seen significant growth in its RNG sales, which now constitute the vast majority of its vehicle fuel sales.
*   **2024 Annual Sales:** The company sold **236.7 million GGEs** of RNG in 2024, representing approximately 89% of its total vehicle fuel sales. This is a massive increase from 13.0 million GGEs in 2013. [4](#ref-4)
*   **2025 Performance:** In the second quarter of 2025, the company sold **61.4 million RNG gallons**. For the first half of 2025, total RNG sales reached **112.0 million gallons**. [1](#ref-1)

### Production Projects and Capacity
The company is actively developing RNG production facilities, often through joint ventures.
*   **Facility Capacity:** Historical data indicates that the company's production facilities (such as those for the Redeem brand) have targeted capacities around **20,000 GGE per day** per facility. [2](#ref-2)
*   **Development Pipelines:** The company continues to invest in new production projects at dairy farms and other organic waste sites to increase its internal supply of RNG. [4](#ref-4)

## 'Redeem' Brand
"Redeem" is Clean Energy's brand for its renewable natural gas fuel.
*   **Definition:** Launched as the first commercially available RNG vehicle fuel, Redeem is derived entirely from organic waste streams such as landfills, dairy farms, and sewage treatment plants. [2](#ref-2) [3](#ref-3)
*   **Carbon Intensity:** According to California Air Resources Board (CARB) estimates, Redeem can reduce carbon emissions by up to **90%** compared to diesel or gasoline. When sourced specifically from dairy waste, it can even have a negative carbon intensity. [2](#ref-2)

## California Station Network
Clean Energy Fuels maintains the largest network of natural gas fueling stations in North America.
*   **California Count:** As of December 31, 2024, the company operates or supplies **over 200 stations in California**. [4](#ref-4)
*   **North American Network:** The total network consists of over 600 stations across the United States and Canada, with 582 stations in 43 states and the District of Columbia as of late 2024. [4](#ref-4)
