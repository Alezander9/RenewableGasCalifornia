# Clean Energy Fuels - Overview

## Market Position in California
Clean Energy Fuels is the largest provider of Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) under the California [Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)](../regulations/lcfs_program.md) program [1](#ref-1). As of the third quarter of 2025, the company reported selling 61.3 million gallons of RNG, representing a 3% increase year-over-year [3](#ref-3).

## Partnerships
Clean Energy Fuels has established several key joint ventures (JV) to expand its RNG production and infrastructure:

*   **TotalEnergies:** A 50/50 joint venture focused on developing carbon-negative RNG production and infrastructure in the United States. TotalEnergies initially provided $100 million in equity, with the potential for this commitment to increase to $400 million [1](#ref-1).
*   **BP:** A 50/50 joint venture dedicated to developing RNG projects at dairy farms. BP initially committed $50 million and Clean Energy Fuels committed $30 million to this partnership [2](#ref-2). This JV followed BP's 2017 acquisition of Clean Energy's upstream RNG business for $155 million [2](#ref-2).
*   **[Maas Energy Works](maas_energy_works.md):** A joint venture focused on the development of dairy RNG projects. As of late 2025, multiple projects under this partnership were either under construction or recently completed [3](#ref-3).
*   **Pioneer Clean Fleet Solutions:** A strategic investment aimed at expanding the company's reach in clean fleet services [3](#ref-3).

## Financial Outlook and LCFS Impact
The company's financial performance is closely tied to the [LCFS](../regulations/lcfs_program.md) program and other carbon credit markets:

*   **LCFS Revenue:** In Q3 2025, Clean Energy Fuels reported $3.1 million in LCFS credit revenue, an increase from $1.9 million in Q3 2024 [3](#ref-3). For the first nine months of 2025, total LCFS revenue reached $9.7 million [3](#ref-3).
*   **Credit Dynamics:** While LCFS credit revenue increased due to higher low-carbon intensity (CI) volumes, total revenue from [RINs](../carbon_credit_markets/rfs_rin_market.md) and LCFS credits combined was $11.4 million in Q3 2025, down from $13.0 million in Q3 2024, primarily due to lower RIN prices [3](#ref-3).
*   **2025 Guidance:** The company has provided a 2025 Adjusted EBITDA guidance range of $60 million to $65 million [4](#ref-4).
*   **2026 Growth Outlook:** Clean Energy Fuels anticipates a "near doubling" of its RNG production in 2026 as new dairy projects become operational, which is expected to be a significant driver of future revenue growth [5](#ref-5).
