# Hydrogen Production (RNG to H2) - Opportunity

The conversion of Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) into hydrogen presents a significant opportunity in California to leverage existing biogas resources for zero-emission transportation and industrial applications.

## Opportunities for RNG to Hydrogen Conversion

### Feedstock Viability
California is actively utilizing RNG derived from dairy biogas and landfill gas as feedstock for hydrogen production. Notable projects include the Aemetis Central Dairy Cluster, which converts biogas to RNG for use in hydrogen fuel cell vehicle (FCEV) applications [[1]](#ref-1).

### Production Methods
*   **Steam Methane Reformation (SMR):** The primary commercial pathway involves SMR using RNG, often facilitated through book-and-claim accounting [[1]](#ref-1).
*   **Advanced Technologies:** Research is ongoing into "Ultra-high Efficiency" electrolytic hydrogen systems designed to upgrade waste hydrogen from RNG/biogas fuel cells [[2]](#ref-2).

### Infrastructure Integration
The California Energy Commission (CEC) is investigating pathways to integrate hydrogen into existing natural gas infrastructure and reduce production costs through various R&D programs [[3]](#ref-3).

## Available Incentives

### Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)
Hydrogen produced from RNG qualifies for LCFS credits under Tier 2 pathways. For instance, liquefied hydrogen produced from landfill-derived RNG can generate credits based on its certified Carbon Intensity (CI) score [[4]](#ref-4).

### Federal Renewable Fuel Standard (RFS2)
RNG-to-hydrogen projects often leverage Renewable Identification Numbers (RINs) under the federal RFS2 framework [[1]](#ref-1).

### State Funding Programs
*   **Low-Carbon Fuel Production Program (LCFPP):** Provides direct incentives for producing low-carbon fuels in California [[1]](#ref-1).
*   **CEC Clean Transportation Program:** Funds hydrogen refueling infrastructure and technology demonstrations [[3]](#ref-3).
*   **Natural Gas Research and Development Program:** Offers grants for projects focused on cost-effective RNG and hydrogen production [[3]](#ref-3).
