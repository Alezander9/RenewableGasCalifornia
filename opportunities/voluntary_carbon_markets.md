# Voluntary Carbon Markets - Opportunity

The voluntary carbon market (VCM) serves as an alternative or complement to compliance-driven markets like the [California Low Carbon Fuel Standard (LCFS)](../carbon_credit_markets/lcfs_overview.md) and the federal [Renewable Fuel Standard (RFS)](../carbon_credit_markets/rfs_rin_market.md) [5](#ref-5) [6](#ref-6).

## RNG Methodologies in the Voluntary Carbon Market

Registry-approved methodologies provide the framework for generating carbon credits from RNG projects in the voluntary market.

*   **Verra (VCS):** The primary methodology for RNG is **VMR0016** (Flaring or Use of Landfill Gas), which replaces previous standards like ACM0001 and AMS-III.G. It covers the capture of landfill gas and its subsequent supply to consumers via natural gas distribution networks, dedicated pipelines, or transport trucks [4](#ref-4).
*   **Gold Standard:** While focused broadly on biogas, Gold Standard offers pathways through methodologies such as "Animal waste management and biogas use for thermal energy generation," providing mechanisms for methane recovery from agricultural and organic waste streams [8](#ref-8).

## Market Dynamics: VCM vs. Compliance Markets (LCFS/RFS)

RNG developers often navigate between compliance and voluntary markets based on pricing, regulatory certainty, and corporate demand.

*   **Pricing Benchmarks:** Historically, the LCFS and RFS (RINs) markets have set the "price to beat" for RNG environmental attributes. RNG developers typically direct their product to the market offering the highest return [6](#ref-6).
*   **Corporate Demand and Premium Pricing:** For voluntary buyers to secure RNG, they must often match or exceed the pricing found in compliance markets. This positions RNG as a premium product in the VCM, driven by corporate net-zero pledges and ESG goals rather than regulatory mandates [5](#ref-5).
*   **Strategic Blending:** Organizations are increasingly utilizing "blended" portfolios that integrate RINs, LCFS credits, and voluntary carbon offsets. This approach allows developers and buyers to manage risk and meet diverse decarbonization targets across different regulatory jurisdictions [7](#ref-7).

## Corporate RNG Credit Purchase Examples

Several major corporations and institutions have integrated Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) into their sustainability strategies through long-term procurement contracts and the purchase of environmental attributes.

#### AstraZeneca
AstraZeneca established a 15-year partnership with [Vanguard Renewables](../companies/vanguard_renewables.md) in June 2023 to supply RNG to its US research and manufacturing sites. This agreement is one of the largest business-to-business RNG purchases in the North American voluntary market, involving 650,000 MMBtu per year. This initiative is part of the company's "Ambition Zero Carbon" program, which targets a 98% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2026 [1](#ref-1) [2](#ref-2).

#### University of California (UC) System
The University of California system entered into a 20-year RNG procurement contract with a median price of approximately $13.73/MMBTU. The UC system's strategy involved selling the associated biomethane attributes ([RINs](../carbon_credit_markets/rfs_rin_market.md) and [LCFS credits](../carbon_credit_markets/lcfs_overview.md)) in the early years of the contract to reduce net costs, with plans to retain these attributes starting in 2025 to achieve its carbon neutrality goals [3](#ref-3).

#### L'Oreal
L'Oreal utilizes long-term RNG procurement for its US operations. Similar to the UC system, L'Oreal has sold RINs and LCFS credits to "buy down" the premium cost of RNG while claiming the displacement of conventional natural gas in its carbon accounting [3](#ref-3).
