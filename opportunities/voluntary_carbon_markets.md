# Voluntary Carbon Markets - Opportunity

## Corporate RNG Credit Purchase Examples

Several major corporations and institutions have integrated Renewable Natural Gas (RNG) into their sustainability strategies through long-term procurement contracts and the purchase of environmental attributes.

#### AstraZeneca
AstraZeneca established a 15-year partnership with Vanguard Renewables in June 2023 to supply RNG to its US research and manufacturing sites. This agreement is one of the largest business-to-business RNG purchases in the North American voluntary market, involving 650,000 MMBtu per year. This initiative is part of the company's "Ambition Zero Carbon" program, which targets a 98% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2026 [[1]](#ref-1) [[2]](#ref-2).

#### University of California (UC) System
The University of California system entered into a 20-year RNG procurement contract with a median price of approximately $13.73/MMBTU. The UC system's strategy involved selling the associated biomethane attributes (RINs and LCFS credits) in the early years of the contract to reduce net costs, with plans to retain these attributes starting in 2025 to achieve its carbon neutrality goals [[3]](#ref-3).

#### L'Oreal
L'Oreal utilizes long-term RNG procurement for its US operations. Similar to the UC system, L'Oreal has sold RINs and LCFS credits to "buy down" the premium cost of RNG while claiming the displacement of conventional natural gas in its carbon accounting [[3]](#ref-3).
